# Government Forms AI Assist

## Allowed capabilities
- Explain a form field.
- Summarize official instructions.
- Detect missing information.
- Validate format and consistency.
- Map guided answers to official field identifiers.
- Generate clearly labeled synthetic training examples.
- Identify uncertainty and route to human review.

## Mandatory verification
- Confirm form and tax/employment year or revision.
- Confirm authoritative source.
- Confirm user role and permissions.
- Confirm submission eligibility.

## Human review required
- Identity attestation.
- Signature.
- Government submission.
- Tax or legal conclusion.
- Employment-authorization determination.
- High-impact correction.

## Prohibited
- Inventing a government form revision.
- Inventing government instructions.
- Fabricating a signature or approval.
- Impersonating a government agency.
- Claiming submission success without an authoritative/provider acknowledgement.
